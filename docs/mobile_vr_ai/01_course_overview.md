## 1. Introduction
With the rapid convergence of immersive technologies and computational intelligence, this course addresses the emerging paradigm where **Mobile Virtual Reality (VR)** meets **Machine Learning (ML)** and **Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)**. 

Mobile VR offers unprecedented accessibility to immersive environments via everyday smartphone hardware and lightweight headsets (e.g., Google Cardboard, Daydream). Coupling this with Artificial Intelligence allows developers and researchers to construct dynamic, adaptive virtual environments populated by intelligent non-player characters (NPCs) capable of perception, decision-making, and goal-directed actions.

---

## 2. Course Objectives
- **Understand VR Fundamentals:** Study human vision, stereoscopy, motion parallax, field of view, and interaction models.
- **Master Modern 3D Engines:** Develop hands-on proficiency in Unity 3D engine rigging, physics, lighting, and scriptable architectures.
- **Learn 3D Content Creation:** Model, UV unwrap, texture, and export game-ready assets in Blender for mobile optimization.
- **Implement Game AI:** Formulate and program discrete pathfinding algorithms ($A^*$) on grid networks.
- **Deploy Machine Learning Models:** Collect human demonstration data for Imitation Learning (Behavioral Cloning).
- **Train Reinforcement Learning Agents:** Formulate Markov Decision Processes (MDP) and train autonomous agents via Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) using Unity ML-Agents.

---

## 3. Prerequisites & Audience
- **Prerequisites:** Basic programming proficiency in C# or Python, foundational understanding of linear algebra and probability.
- **Target Audience:** Undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students (Computer Science, Electrical, AI, Mechanical), game developers, and XR researchers.
